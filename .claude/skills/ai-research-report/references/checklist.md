# Submission checklist

Record results and unresolved items in `99_review.md`.

## Evidence and results

- [ ] Every number matches a source result, table, or figure.
- [ ] Regenerated figures/tables match draft values.
- [ ] Seed/run count and baseline provenance are clear.
- [ ] Every comparative claim has evidence; confidence matches wording.
- [ ] Regressions, uneven gains, and failed cases remain visible.
- [ ] Missing ablations and experimental details are disclosed.

## Content

- [ ] Abstract gives method and concrete supported result.
- [ ] Introduction contributions align with Method and Experiments.
- [ ] Limitations describe real scope, assumptions, and relevant risks.
- [ ] Dataset, split, hyperparameters, hardware, seed, and runtime stated when available; unavailable metadata is not guessed.
- [ ] No unsupported “significant”, “groundbreaking”, “unprecedented”, or “state-of-the-art” claim.

## Citations

- [ ] Every cited work verified against primary source.
- [ ] Every `\\cite{}` key exists in `references.bib`; every bibliography entry is cited.
- [ ] Entries include verified title, authors, year, and venue.
- [ ] No unresolved `[CẦN XÁC MINH]` remains in final draft.
- [ ] Claims about papers are based on inspected source, not title alone.

## Figures and format

- [ ] Each figure/table is referenced; captions in correct position.
- [ ] Figures are vector where feasible, readable, and colorblind-safe.
- [ ] Tables use booktabs, no vertical rules, and clear metric/run captions.
- [ ] Official venue template, page limit, anonymization, AI policy, ethics, and artifact rules checked.
- [ ] Compilation succeeds without undefined citations/references.
- [ ] Final PDF inspected for layout, floating figures, overflow, and legibility.
